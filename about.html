Our Story

Built by people who wanted better payroll software.

Founded in 2014 by small-business owners to make payroll the most reliable, worry-free part of running your business.

Our Mission

Make payroll a non-event.

Meridian Payroll started when our founders, both former small-business owners, got fed up with providers that treated payroll as an afterthought. We built the company we wished we had.

Payroll should be the most boring, dependable part of running a business. Every single product decision we make is measured against that standard.

Accuracy First

Every calculation is verified before it ever reaches a paycheck or tax filing.

People Over Tickets

You get a named specialist, not a rotating support queue.

Plain-Language Compliance

We explain the "why" behind every rule, not just the rule itself.

Milestones

A decade of getting payroll right.

Key milestones that shaped our journey from a startup to a trusted nationwide payroll provider.

2014

Founded in a co-working space

Two former small-business owners start Meridian to fix the payroll experience they'd struggled with themselves.

2017

100th client & multi-state expansion

Meridian adds multi-state payroll support as clients expand beyond their home state.

2020

SOC 2 Type II certification achieved

Independent audit confirms our security and compliance controls meet enterprise standards.

2026

2,400+ businesses served

Meridian now processes payroll across all 50 states for businesses from 5 to 500 employees.
