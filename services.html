Our Services

Payroll, tax and benefits — fully managed.

Three tightly integrated services that replace the spreadsheets, filing deadlines and benefits admin that eat up your week.

01 · Payroll Processing

Every pay run, calculated right, on time.

We run payroll on your schedule — weekly, biweekly, semi-monthly or monthly — across hourly, salaried and contractor pay types, with garnishments, bonuses and multi-state rules handled automatically.

  • Direct deposit & pay cardsFunds land on payday, every time, with same-day options available.
  • Multi-state & multi-entity payrollCorrect withholding rules applied automatically by work location.
  • Garnishments & deductionsCourt orders, child support and benefits deductions calculated without manual entry.
02 · Tax Compliance

Filed, paid, and guaranteed — no exceptions.

Federal, state and local payroll taxes are calculated, filed and remitted on your behalf. If we make a filing error, we cover the resulting penalty — that's our compliance guarantee.

  • Automatic tax filingsForm 941, 940, state unemployment and local filings submitted for you.
  • New hire & tax account registrationWe handle state tax account setup when you expand to a new state.
  • Year-end W-2s & 1099sPrepared, filed and distributed to employees and contractors automatically.
03 · Benefits Administration

Benefits that sync with payroll automatically.

Health insurance, dental, vision, retirement plans and PTO policies connect directly to your payroll run — no more reconciling deduction spreadsheets by hand.

  • Open enrollment supportGuided annual enrollment with employee self-service portal access.
  • Retirement plan integration401(k) and similar plan contributions synced automatically each pay run.
  • PTO & leave trackingAccruals calculated and visible to employees and managers alike.
Integrations

Works with the tools you already use.

Meridian connects to your accounting, time-tracking and HR systems, so payroll data flows without double entry.

Accounting Sync

General ledger entries post automatically after every payroll run, matched to your chart of accounts.

Time & Attendance

Hours from your time-tracking system import directly into each pay run — no manual re-entry.

HRIS Connections

Employee records, org charts and onboarding data stay in sync between your HR system and payroll.

How Delivery Works

What a pay cycle looks like with Meridian.

1

Submit hours

Approve hours and any changes through your dashboard before the cutoff.

2

We calculate

Gross-to-net pay, taxes and deductions are calculated and flagged for review.

3

You approve

A one-click approval confirms the run — or request changes before it's final.

4

We process

Direct deposits, tax remittances and reports are handled automatically.
